Hi! My name is Caitlin M. Allen and I am a security operations analyst based in the San Francisco East Bay. [I graduated in May of 2021 with my Bachelor's in Computer Networking & Cybersecurity with a minor in Computer & Digital Forensics from Champlain College in Burlington, Vermont](https://www.champlain.edu/directory/caitlin-allen/). While at Champlain, I was the Communications Lead for [Womxn in Technology](https://www.champlain.edu/clubs-activities-affinity-groups/womxn-in-technology/) and a member of Cybersecurity Club and Digital Forensics Association.  

After my graduation, I transitioned from an intern to a threat analyst at a Vermont-based cybersecurity company. In my current role, I still apply the skills and knowledge I gained to help strengthen my company's security posture and enhance our security program. While my title is no longer threat analyst, I still love to shadow and help out the threat intel team to learn as much as possible.  

While I absolutely love the world of cybersecurity, I also am an avid dressage rider who took time off from competing to complete my undergrad. Throughout high school, I was a competing member of the United States Equestrian Federation ([USEF](https://www.usef.org/)), United States Dressage Federation ([USDF](https://usdf.org/)), and Eastern States Dressage and Combined Training Association ([ESDCTA](https://esdcta.org/)). In 2017, I was awarded the [USDF Region 1](http://www.usdfreg1.org/wp/) Youth Volunteer of the Year award. 

As of May 2025, I am a Director at Large for the Metropolitan Horsemen’s Association ([MHA](https://www.mhaeastbay.org/)) based in Oakland, California. While putting competing and my dreams of my USDF medals and freestyle bars on hold to keep furthering my career, I am excited to be apart of an organization with deep roots in Oakland’s history!
